# Product Branding
## RetailVision AI

---

## 1. Product Name
**RetailVision AI**

## 2. Tagline
"Decoding Customer Behavior. Optimizing Retail Space."

## 3. Mission
To provide retailers with the granular, actionable intelligence needed to optimize store layouts, improve customer experience, and increase conversion rates, all powered by privacy-conscious AI.

## 4. Vision
To become the industry standard operating system for physical retail analytics, seamlessly merging the physical and digital retail experience through spatial intelligence.

## 5. Target Audience
- Retail Store Managers looking to optimize product placement.
- Retail Operations Directors analyzing multi-store performance.
- Merchandising Teams testing layout effectiveness.
- Marketing Teams measuring engagement with promotional endcaps.

## 6. Value Proposition
Traditional retail analytics stop at the door (footfall counting) or the register (POS data). RetailVision AI illuminates the "blind spot" in between by analyzing how customers move, where they dwell, and exactly how they interact with products on the shelf.

## 7. Brand Personality
- **Analytical & Precise:** Data-driven, exact, and trustworthy.
- **Modern & Premium:** Reflects enterprise-grade, cutting-edge technology.
- **Action-Oriented:** Focuses on outcomes and recommendations, not just raw data.
- **Unobtrusive:** Operates quietly in the background (privacy-first).

## 8. Tone of Voice
- **Professional but Accessible:** Avoids overly dense academic AI jargon; speaks the language of retail business outcomes.
- **Confident:** Backs up recommendations with hard data.
- **Concise:** respects the user's time.

## 9. Logo Concept
- A minimalist, geometric logo combining a stylized camera aperture or eye with a subtle bar chart or data node structure.
- Clean lines, matching the 2px stroke weight of the UI icons.

## 10. Brand Colors
- **Primary Brand Color:** Indigo (`#4F46E5` / `#6366F1`) - Signifies intelligence, technology, and trust.
- **Secondary Brand Color:** Slate / Charcoal - Provides a sophisticated, serious grounding for the UI and marketing materials.
