## 1. Tracking Status

## 2. Explanation

Tracking was not implemented for two reasons:
1. **Explicitly Out of Scope:** The Phase 1 requirements strictly stated: "Do NOT implement Tracking. Detection only."
2. **Missing Prerequisite:** Tracking algorithms (such as ByteTrack or DeepSORT) require bounding box detections as their foundational input. Since the Person Detection Engine itself was not implemented in this phase, it is impossible to evaluate any tracking metrics (Lost Tracks, ID Switching, Track Stability).
