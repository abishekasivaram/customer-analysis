# Phase 6 – AI Video Intelligence Workspace & Business Intelligence
## RetailVision AI

---

# Phase Objective

Phase 6 transforms RetailVision AI from an analytics dashboard into a complete AI-powered retail intelligence platform.

Unlike previous phases that generated analytics, this phase focuses on delivering an end-to-end enterprise workflow where a retailer can upload surveillance footage, monitor AI processing in real time, visualize customer behavior as it happens, review AI-generated business intelligence, replay annotated evidence, and export comprehensive reports.

This phase represents the complete user experience and should become the primary demonstration during HackZen.

---

# Phase Principles

- Use ONLY real outputs generated by Phases 1–5.
- Do NOT use mock data.
- Do NOT generate placeholder analytics.
- Every visualization must originate from the AI pipeline.
- Maintain enterprise SaaS design consistency established in Phase 5.
- The application should resemble commercial AI analytics platforms such as Microsoft Fabric, Palantir Foundry, Datadog, and Stripe Dashboard.
